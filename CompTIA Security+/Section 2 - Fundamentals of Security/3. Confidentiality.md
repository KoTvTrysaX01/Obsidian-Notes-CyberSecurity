The protection of information from unauthorized access

``Confidentiality is about keeping sensitive information out of the wrong hands while still letting the right hands work with it
## Why Confidentiality Matters?
* Personal Privacy. To protect personal privacy
* Business Advantage. To maintain a competitive business advantage
* Regulatory Compliance. To comply with laws and regulations*
	* [[HIPPA]] protects health information
	* [[PCI DSS]] protects credit card data
	* [[GDPR]] protects the personal data of European citizens
## Five Common Methods to Achieve Confidentiality
1. [[Encryption]]: Scrambles readable plaintext into unreadable ciphertext using a mathematical algorithm and a key.
	* Data at Rest (Hard drives)
	* Data in Transit (HTTPS Connections)
2. [[Access controls]]: The rules and mechanisms that determine who can see what.
	* Usernames
	* Passwords
	* Multi-Factor Authentication
	* Permission Lists
3. [[Data masking]]: Replaces sensitive data with characters that have the same format but reveal nothing.
	* Passwords
	* Credit Cards
	* DNIs
4. [[Physical security]]: Protects the tangible objects that hold or display our data.
	* Locks on server room doors
	* Badge readers in the data center
	* Locked filing cabinets for paper records
	* Privacy screens on laptops used in public places
5. [[Training and Awareness]]: People are the weakest link in most security programs.
	* Phishing emails
	* Leave passwords on sticky notes
	* Accidentally email a spreadsheet to the wrong recipient
## Key Takeaway
* Confidentiality is the principle that information is accessible only to thos who are authorized to access it.
* Achieve confidentiality through encryption, access controls, data masking, physical security, or training
