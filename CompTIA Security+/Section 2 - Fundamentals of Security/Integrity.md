The data has not been altered, tampered with, or destroyed by anyone or anything that wasn't authorized to make the change.
Data integrity means data remains unchanged since creation or legitimate modification.
## Why Integrity Matters?
* To ensure data accuracy
*  To maintain trust
* To keep systems operating correctly
### Data Accuracy
It is about whether the data is unchanged from its original form
### Trust
Maintaining integrity is what makes a digital system credible
### System Operability
Modern systems make automated decisions based on data.
Tempered data can cause catastrophic decisions.
## Five Primary Methods to Maintain Integrity
1. [[Hashing]]
2. [[Digital signatures]]
3. [[Checksums]]
4. [[Access controls]]
5. [[Regular audits]]
# Key Takeaway
* Integrity ensures that data remains consistent, accurate, and unaltered throughout its lifecycle
* [[Hashing]] is the primary verification tool
