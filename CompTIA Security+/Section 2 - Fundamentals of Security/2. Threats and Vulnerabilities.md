## [[Threat]]
Anything or anyone that has the potential to cause harm to a system.
A possibility, not a guarantee.

Can be intentional or unintentional.

We generally cannot eliminate threats, but we can anticipate them and put controls in place to reduce the damage they cause.

## [[Vulnerability]]
A weakness in a system, a process, or a person that a threat can exploit.

Unlike threats, vulnerabilities are usually within out control.

## [[Risk]]
The calculated likelihood that a specific threat will successfully exploit a specific vulnerability, combined with the impact if it does.

![[Pasted image 20261127141144.png]]

**Risk = Likelihood x Impact**

If you have a threat but no matching vulnerability, you have a very little risk

If you have a vulnerability but no threat targeting it, you also have very little risk

Risk lives at the intersection of the threat and the vulnerability

1. Every decision is a risk management decision
2. Budget, staff, and time are limited
3. Not every vulnerability or threat can be addressed 
4. Prioritize the highest-risk threat and vulnerability combination first

## Four basic Response Option
1. Mitigate it
2. Transfer it
3. Avoid it
4. Accept it

## Key Takeaway
* A Threat is a weakness
* A threat is something or someone that can exploit that weakness
* A risk is the calculated combination of how likely the exploit is and how bad it would be if it actually happened*
* Risk = Likelihood x Impact
* Managing risk is the core of cybersecurity
