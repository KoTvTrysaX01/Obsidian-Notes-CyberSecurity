Having undeniable, verifiable proof that a specific person or system performed a specific action.
Proves the sender and that the content has not changed since signing.
## [[Digital signatures]]
Created by hashing a message and encrypting the hash with the sender's private key.
Private key only known to the sender decrypting the signature with the matching public key proves the message came from that sender and was not altered in transit.
## Three Reasons Non-Repudiation Matters
* Confirms the authenticity of digital transactions
* Reinforces integrity
* Creates accountability
## Secure Audit Trail
A tamper-evident log recording logins, access, and changes, creating a trail that cannot be easily denied later.

>Audit logs and digital signatures work together to deliver enterprise-wide non-repudiation.
# Key Takeaway
* Non-repudiation ensures undeniable proof of participation in a digital action
* Non-repudiation is primarly achieved through [[Digital signatures]]
* With audit trails, non-repudiation gives digital interaction legal and operational weight
